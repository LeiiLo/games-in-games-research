# DevilutionX: a rebuilt Diablo engine

**Repository:** [diasurgical/DevilutionX@dac104b](https://github.com/diasurgical/DevilutionX/tree/dac104babfb6187415432f428ac2516747ffc154) (default branch, 6 Oct 2026)  
**Route:** engine recreation (host for a mechanic mod)  
**What we read:** the parts the movement mod touches. Nothing was run.  
**AI credit (as the project states it):** recent commits co-authored by Claude models.

A long-running community rebuild of the Diablo engine, and the host for the [Diablo II movement mod](devilutionx-d2-movement.md).

**What we learned:** a mechanic mod inside a rebuilt host engine is the smallest kind of "game in a game". The host's tile occupancy, combat and saves stay in charge, and the mod changes only movement. Recent commits are co-authored by AI models, but the project's foundations are years of human work; keep that history in view when you credit it.
