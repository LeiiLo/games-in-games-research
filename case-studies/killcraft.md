# Killcraft: Minecraft inside ULTRAKILL

**Repository:** [goonsn/Killcraft@ce05bae](https://github.com/goonsn/Killcraft/tree/ce05bae49a32a3a6c93b7f33acae73d2be05de15) (default branch, 6 Oct 2026)

**What we read:** README. Nothing was run.  
**AI credit (as the project states it):** commits co-authored by Claude.

A hidden Minecraft runs SkyCraft's mod in the background while ULTRAKILL shows the player Minecraft's movement, health, inventory and combat on its levels.

**What we learned:** SkyCraft's guest side carries over to a very different host; the per-host work is in the host plugin. Its unit choice (2 host units per block) is another data point for writing units into the contract.
